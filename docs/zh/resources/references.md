# 参考文献

本文档列出 Bookmarks Cleaner 设计和实现过程中参考的学术论文、技术文章和开源项目。

## 书签管理

### 学术论文

1. **Kumar, R., & Tomkins, A. (2010)**. "A Characterization of Online Search Behavior". *IEEE Data Engineering Bulletin*, 33(1), 3-11.
   - 分析用户在线搜索和书签保存行为模式

2. **Millen, D. R., & Feinberg, J. (2006)**. "Using Social Tagging to Improve Social Navigation". *Workshop on the Social Navigation and Community based Adaptation Technologies*.
   - 探讨社交标签在内容组织中的应用

### 技术文章

3. **Mozilla Developer Network**. "Browser Bookmark File Format". [MDN Web Docs](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Places)
   - 浏览器书签文件格式规范

## 文本分类

### 学术论文

4. **Zhang, X., Zhao, J., & LeCun, Y. (2015)**. "Character-level Convolutional Networks for Text Classification". *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems*, 28.
   - 字符级卷积网络在文本分类中的应用

5. **Joulin, A., et al. (2017)**. "Bag of Tricks for Efficient Text Classification". *Proceedings of the 15th Conference of the European Chapter of the Association for Computational Linguistics*.
   - FastText 高效文本分类方法

6. **Devlin, J., et al. (2019)**. "BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers for Language Understanding". *Proceedings of NAACL-HLT*.
   - BERT 预训练模型在文本理解中的应用

### 技术文章

7. **Scikit-learn Documentation**. "Text Feature Extraction". [scikit-learn.org](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/feature_extraction.html#text-feature-extraction)
   - 文本特征提取方法详解
