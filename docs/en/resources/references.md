# References

This document lists academic papers, technical articles, and open source projects referenced during the design and implementation of Bookmarks Cleaner.

## Bookmark Management

1. **Kumar, R., & Tomkins, A. (2010)**. "A Characterization of Online Search Behavior". *IEEE Data Engineering Bulletin*.

2. **Millen, D. R., & Feinberg, J. (2006)**. "Using Social Tagging to Improve Social Navigation".

## Text Classification

3. **Zhang, X., Zhao, J., & LeCun, Y. (2015)**. "Character-level Convolutional Networks for Text Classification". *NIPS*.

4. **Joulin, A., et al. (2017)**. "Bag of Tricks for Efficient Text Classification". *EACL*. (FastText)

5. **Devlin, J., et al. (2019)**. "BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers". *NAACL-HLT*.

## Ensemble Learning

6. **Kuncheva, L. I. (2004)**. "Combining Pattern Classifiers: Methods and Algorithms". *Wiley*.

7. **Zhou, Z. H. (2012)**. "Ensemble Methods: Foundations and Algorithms". *CRC Press*.

## Semantic Analysis

8. **Mikolov, T., et al. (2013)**. "Distributed Representations of Words and Phrases". *NIPS*. (Word2Vec)

9. **Reimers, N., & Gurevych, I. (2019)**. "Sentence-BERT: Sentence Embeddings using Siamese BERT-Networks". *EMNLP*.

## Large Language Models

10. **Brown, T., et al. (2020)**. "Language Models are Few-Shot Learners". *NIPS*. (GPT-3)

11. **Wei, J., et al. (2022)**. "Chain-of-Thought Prompting Elicits Reasoning in Large Language Models". *NIPS*.

## Software Architecture

12. **Martin, R. C. (2017)**. "Clean Architecture: A Craftsman's Guide to Software Structure and Design". *Prentice Hall*.
